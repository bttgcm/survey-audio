An exploratory survey on IMS and flying

In recent years, growing public awareness of the environmental impact of air travel has contributed to increased discussion about the environmental implications of flying and the extent to which individuals should reconsider their travel choices. These concerns are reflected in ideas associated with flight shame (or flygskam), which encourage people to reflect on the environmental consequences of flying and consider more sustainable alternatives where feasible.

This survey aims to explore how MSF International Mobile Staff (IMS) perceive and experience these concerns in the context of their professional mobility, including how they relate to their personal views and the requirements of their work.
